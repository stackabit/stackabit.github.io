Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Stackabit is a supplement reminder app: you add your supplements, and Stackabit sets the times, grouping and warnings. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible

The app is published by Emre Dereli, an individual developer based in Türkiye (the "data controller"). Contact: stackabit.destek@gmail.com.

2. What we process

  • Account: an anonymous user ID created when you start. If you create an account: your email address, or the identifier that Apple or Google gives us (Apple may provide a private relay address).
  • Your routine: wake-up, meal and sleep times; training days, start times and durations.
  • Your supplements: supplement types, brands and product names, doses and units, schedules, stock amounts and personal notes you write.
  • Intake records: which doses you marked as taken or skipped, and when.
  • Subscription status: whether your subscription is active (see RevenueCat and Apple below). We never see your payment card.

Supplement and intake information can reveal something about your health. We treat it as sensitive and use it only to run the app for you.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • The app does not track calories, macros or food.
  • The app does not recommend doses. Doses come from your product label and what you enter.

4. Why we process it, and the legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you asked for: schedules, reminders, stock, history and statistics, and keeping your data when you change phones. Basis: performance of the contract (providing the app), and your explicit consent for health-related information, which you give by entering it. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data or your account.

5. Who else handles data

  • Supabase (database and sign-in). Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • RevenueCat (subscription management). It receives your app user ID and purchase status. It does not receive your supplements.
  • Apple (App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, TestFlight) and Google (Sign in with Google, if you use it). They act under their own privacy policies.
  • Open Food Facts (open product database). If you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent from your phone to look up the product, so Open Food Facts can see your IP address and that number. The camera is used only to read the barcode; pictures are not stored or uploaded.
  • Email delivery: an email service sends you the 6-digit sign-in code if you choose email sign-in.

Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent from our servers.

6. Storage on your device

The app stores your language, theme, a cached copy of your latest data (so it opens offline) and unsent changes on your phone. Signing out or deleting your account removes this cache.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you use Settings → Delete account, your account and all data linked to it are deleted from our database immediately. Copies in the provider's backups may remain for a limited time before they are overwritten. Anonymous accounts that never finish setup are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

8. Your rights

Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 11): you can learn whether your data is processed and for what purpose, ask for information about it, learn who it is shared with, request correction, request deletion or destruction, ask that these actions are notified to third parties, object to a result against you that comes from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR): you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

The quickest way: delete data or your account in the app, or email stackabit.destek@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. In Türkiye you can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

9. Children

Stackabit is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each user can only read their own data (row-level access rules). No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect to what the app needs.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where needed, tell you in the app.
